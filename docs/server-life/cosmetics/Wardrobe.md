# What is the wardrobe? 
The wardrobe `/wardrobe`, allows you to view cosmetics you've unlocked, and **some you haven't**, you can customize and style your character ready for play!

If you don't want to preview your options you can open the menu with just `/cosmetic`, which will allow you to choose:
- Hats
- Backpacks
- Offhand
- Balloons
**without taking up an armour slot**, this means you can still wear your helmet even if you have a hat on!
Which is particular useful when fighting bosses.

# Types of Cosmetics
There are four key types of cosmetics on the server, 
- Hats: These go on your head, and can vary in style, you start with a "frog hat", to give you an idea of how it works!
- Backpack: These cosmetic items go on your back, and come in all sorts of shapes and sizes, from simple explorers backpack to custom wings.
- Offhand: These cosmetics show in your offhand
- Balloons: Cute balloons attached to you via a lead, that follow you where you go, a cool way to stand out amongst a crowd

Cosmetics, as the name suggests are **purely cosmetic**, these add no extra utilities, for custom weapons *that do* give you custom things you should check out Wraps and Boss fights to find out more.
